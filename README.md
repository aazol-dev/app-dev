# Project Hail Mary
## Movie Information
**Title:** Project Hail Mary
**Genre:** Science Fiction and Adventure
**Author:** Andy Weir
**Main Actor:** Ryan Gosling

---

## Movie Summary

*Project Hail Mary* is a science fiction movie about Ryland Grace, a teacher who wakes up alone on a spaceship. He discovers that he has an important mission to save Earth from a mysterious threat.

### Main Themes
- Friendship
- Teamwork
- Courage
- Science and Discovery

## My Opinion

I think this movie is interesting because it combines science, adventure, and friendship. It teaches us the importance of teamwork and problem-solving.
